# Free fax cover sheet template

A plain, printable fax cover sheet you can fill in and print or save as a PDF. No sign-up, no tracking, works offline.

Two versions:

- `fax-cover-sheet.html`: open it in any browser, click a field to type, then print (Ctrl+P or Cmd+P) or choose "Save as PDF". Nothing you type leaves your computer.
- `fax-cover-sheet.txt`: a plain-text version for people who prefer to fill it in by hand or paste it into a document.

Prefer not to download anything? The same form runs in the browser at [confirmfax.com/fax-cover-sheet](https://www.confirmfax.com/fax-cover-sheet).

## What goes on a fax cover sheet

- The recipient's name, company or department, and fax number
- Your name, phone number and (if you have one) your fax number
- The date
- The total number of pages, counting the cover sheet, so the person receiving it can check nothing is missing
- A subject line, such as a case, claim or account number
- A short message and, if needed, a confidentiality note

Most offices, including doctors' offices, courts and HR departments, expect a cover sheet. It tells the front desk who the fax is for so it doesn't sit in the tray.
